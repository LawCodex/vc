### 1.

The Permitted Use of Cannabis for Religious Purposes
Bill, 2018

An Act to provide for the decriminalization of the use of
cannabis as a sacrament in adherence to a religious
practice by such religious bodies as may be prescribed by
Order of the Minister, including, but not limited to, the
Rastafarian faith, at their place of worship and at an event
declared by Order of the Minster to be an exempt event,
for the purposes of this Act and for matters and purposes
incidental thereto.
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the House
of Assembly of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and by
the authority of the same, as follows:Short title and commencement.
This Act may be cited as the Permitted Use of Cannabis
for Religious Purposes Act, 2018 and shall come into operation
on such day to be appointed by the Minister by notice published
in the Gazette.

Permitted use of Cannabis for religious purposes.

### 3.

(1) Subject to an Order made by the Minister under section
4 and except as otherwise provided for under this Act, a
person who is an adherent of a religious body, including, but
not limited to, the Rastafarian faith, or an organization
comprising of such persons, may3

(a) cultivate cannabis to be used solely for religious
purposes as a sacrament in adherence to a religious
practice of the religious body, on lands designated by
Order of the Minister,
(b) Possess cannabis, which is less than or equal to such
amount as may be specified by the Minister by Order, to
be used solely for religious purposes as a sacrament in
adherence to a religious practice of the religious body;
(c) transport cannabis which is cultivated for religious
purposes, to a place of worship or pursuant to section
10, at an event declared by Order of the Minster to be an
exempt event for the purposes of this Act, in compliance
with such guidelines as may be issued by the Minister by
Notice published in the Gazette, for use as a sacrament in
adherence to a religious practice of a religious body;
and
(d) supply and use cannabis for religious purposes as a
sacrament in adherence to a religious practice of a
religious body, at a place of worship or pursuant to
section 10, at an event declared by Order of the Minster
to be an exempt event for the purposes of this Act.
(2) For the avoidance of doubt, where there are reasonable
grounds to suspect that there is an intention to contravene the
provisions of section 6 or 7 or 8 of the Drugs (Prevention of
Misuse) Act, subsection (1) shall not restrict the exercise of
the discretion of the Director of Public Prosecutions from
prosecuting a person for a relevant offence under that Act or

any other relevant enactment.
Application for designation by Minister

### 4.

(1) Pursuant to section 3, a person who is member of a
religious body which proposes to use cannabis for religious
purposes, as a sacrament in adherence to a religious practice
of the religious body, shall apply to the Minister, in the
prescribed manner, to have the body designated as a religious
body for the purposes of this Act.
(2) An application under subsection (1) shall include (a) the name, address and telephone number of the person
or persons appointed as leaders of the religious body;
(b) the address of the place of worship of the religious
body;
(c) a survey plan of the land on which cannabis for use in
accordance with this Act is to be cultivated; and
(d) the proposed arrangements for the transportation of the
cannabis from the land on which it is proposed to be
cultivated, to the place of worship.
(3) Where the Minister is satisfied that a religious body will
comply with the provisions of this Act and any regulations or
orders made hereunder, he shall approve the application and
cause to be published in the Gazette, an Order(a) designating 5

(i) the body as a religious body that is authorized to
cultivate, possess, transport, supply and use
cannabis for religious purposes in accordance with
the provisions of this Act;
(ii) the place of worship of the religious body, as a
place of worship where the members of the
religious body are authorized to cultivate, possess,
supply and use cannabis for religious purposes in
accordance with the provisions of this Act;
(b) designating the lands specified in the Order, as
lands to be used for the cultivation of cannabis for
religious purposes in accordance with the
provisions of this Act; and
(c) authorizing the members of the religious body to use
cannabis for religious purposes, at a place of worship,
in accordance with the provisions of this Act or
pursuant to section 10, at an event declared by Order
of the Minister to be an exempt event for the purposes
of this Act.
Cultivation of cannabis by a religious body

### 5.

Only the prescribed amount of cannabis shall be cultivated
by a religious body in the prescribed manner and the lands
upon which the cannabis is cultivated shall be subject to such
inspections, as the Minister may deem necessary, so as to

ensure compliance with the provisions of this Act or
regulations made pursuant to this Act.
Revocation of Order

### 6.

(1) The Minister may revoke an Order made under this
Act if any of the conditions of the Order or the provisions of
this Act is contravened by the members the religious body.
(2) Where an Order is revoked pursuant to subsection (2), the
Minister may order that all cannabis that is cultivated
cultivated by the religious body shall be seized and destroyed
by the Authority.
Prohibition of importation of cannabis.

### 7.

No person shall import cannabis into St. Vincent and the
Grenadines for religious purposes.
Possession of cannabis authorised for religious use.

### 8.

The possession of cannabis by any person who is an
adherent of a religious faith, at a place worship or, pursuant to
section 10, at an event declared by order of the Minister, to be
an exempt event for the purposes of this Act, is hereby
authorized for the purposes of this Act and no person shall be
liable to arrest, detention or prosecution for any offence, or
any other penalty, as regards cannabis being transported to, or
which is possessed, supplied or used at the place of worship
or such event in such amount and subject to such conditions
as may be prescribed.

Use of cannabis only at place of worship or at exempt
event

### 9.

(1) The use of cannabis by any person under this Act shall
only be at and in relation to, a place of worship or, pursuant
to section 10, an event declared by Order of the Minister to be
an exempt event for the purposes of this Act.
(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an
offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding fifty thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment
not exceeding six months or to both a fine and imprisonment.
Declaration of exempt event

### 10.

(1) The Minister may, by Order, declare an event to be an
exempt event for the purposes of this Act, if he is satisfied
that the event is(a) promoted or sponsored by a person who is an
adherent of a prescribed religious body; and
(b) primarily for religious purposes, in accordance with
the provisions of this Act.
(2) Where an event is declared under this section to be an
exempt event for the purposes of this Act, no person shall be
liable to arrest, detention or prosecution for any offence or
any other penalty as regards cannabis being transported to, or
which is possessed, supplied or used at the exempt event in

such amount and under such conditions as are specified in the
Order.
Prohibition of use of cannabis by minors.

### 11.

(1) No person shall permit a minor to use cannabis at a
place of worship or at an exempt event.
(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits a
summary offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not
exceeding fifty thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment
not exceeding six months.
Issuance of guidelines by the Minister

### 12.

The Minister may, by Notice published in the Gazette,
issue guidelines to assist in the effective operation of this Act.
Regulations.

### 13.

The Minister shall make Regulations for or with respect
to any matter that, by this Act, is required or permitted to be
prescribed or that is necessary or convenient to be prescribed
for carrying out or giving effect to this Act.
Objects and Reasons
This Bill seeks to provide for decriminalization and not
legalization of cannabis for religious purposes to be used
solely as a sacrament in adherence to a religious practice of a
religious body. Such use is permitted only at places of
worship by religious bodies, including, but not limited to the

Rastafarian faith. The Bill also authorizes the use of cannabis
for religious purposes at events that are declared by the
Minister by Order to be an exempt event for the purposes of
the Act.
