BILL FOR
ACT NO.

OF 2007

I ASSENT

[L.S.]
AN ACT to provide for the establishment of a regulatory framework that would facilitate
the growth of the small business sector in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.
[ ]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the House of Assembly of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and
by the authority of the same as follows:1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Small Business Development Act, 2007.
(2) This Act shall come into operation on a date appointed by the GovernorGeneral by Proclamation in the Gazette.
2\. (1) In this Act :
“approved private sector organisation” means a private sector organisation
approved by Cabinet pursuant to section 11;
“approved small business” means a micro or small enterprise approved by
Cabinet pursuant to section 6 (1);
“controlled” has the meaning assigned to it by section 539 of the Companies Act;
“micro enterprise” means a small business:
(a) in which not more than five persons are employed;
(b) whose gross revenue does not exceed one hundred thousand dollars per
year;
(c) whose net assets do not exceed seventy-five thousand dollars per year; and

(d) which, if it is a company, has more than 75 per cent of its shares owned
locally;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for commerce;
“Register” means the Register of Approved Small Businesses;
“Registrar” means the Registrar responsible for commerce;
“small enterprise” means a small business:
(a) in which not more than fifty persons are employed;
(b) whose gross revenue does not exceed one million dollars per year;
(c) whose net assets do not exceed half a million dollars per year; and
(d) which, if it is a company, has more than 75 per cent of its shares owned
locally.
(2) For the purposes of this section, local ownership of shares includes ownership
by the member States of the Caribbean Community excluding an Associate Member
within the meaning of Article 231 of the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas.
3\. (1) A micro or small enterprise may be eligible for approved small business status
under this Act if:
(a) it is engaged in any of the activities listed in the First Schedule and it
offers or is likely to offer significant or substantial socio-economic
benefits to Saint Vincent and the Grenadines; and
(b) it is not a party to an agreement for the payment of fees on a continuing
basis for managerial or other services to persons who are not nationals of
Saint Vincent and the Grenadines or other member States of the Caribbean
Community and who are not resident in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines
or any other member State of the Caribbean Community where those
services do not form part of the normal business operations of the
enterprise; and
(c) it is not a company or is registered under the Registration of Business
Names Act; or
(d) in the case of a company, it is incorporated or registered under the
Companies Act; or
(e) it is owned or controlled by a company, whose gross revenue and net
assets do not exceed the gross revenue and net assets mentioned in section
2 of this Act.
(2) For the purposes of subsection (1):
(a) “Caribbean Community” has the meaning assigned to it by the revised
Treaty of Chaguaramas establishing the Caribbean Community including
the CARICOM Single Market and Economy signed at Nassau, The
Bahamas on 5th July 2001;

(b) “local ownership of shares” includes ownership by the member States of
the Caribbean Community but does not include ownership by an Associate
Member within the meaning of Article 231 of the Revised Treaty of
Chaguaramas;
(c) “socio-economic benefit” means(i) the generation of new investment or the development of products or
processes,
(ii) an improvement in employment or production capacity through market
research, technical invention or innovation, or
(iii)the enhancement of export potential, foreign exchange earnings or
savings, or the general welfare of persons in Saint Vincent and the
Grenadines.
4\. (1) A small business shall not be entitled to apply for or qualify to receive any
incentives under this Act if it receives fiscal incentives under the Fiscal Incentives Act.
(2) Where a small business is one of a group of companies or is controlled by
another company, only one small business may be eligible to receive incentives under
this Act.
5\. (1) A micro or small enterprise that wishes to become an approved small business
shall make an application to the Minister for the consideration of Cabinet.
(2) An application made under subsection (1) shall be in Form I of the Second
Schedule and shall be accompanied:
(a) by a certificate of registration issued under the Registration of Business
Names Act, where applicable;
(b) by a certificate of incorporation issued under the Companies Act, where
applicable;
(c) by a declaration verifying the particulars specified in subsection (3);
(d) by a business plan; and
(e) by the audited financial statement of accounts for the financial year
immediately preceding that in which the application is made, where the
business has been in operation prior to the commencement of this Act.
(3) The particulars referred to in subsection (2) (c) are:
(a) the name and address of the business, its legal nature and the name,
residential address and occupation of each partner, individual, shareholder
or director, as the case may be;
(b) the interest in partnership property of each partner, or the number of
shares held by each shareholder in the company to which the application
relates, as the case may be;

(c) the names of all other partnerships or companies in which the partner,
shareholders or directors referred to in paragraph (a) are also partners,
shareholders or directors, and the extent of any such interest or
shareholding, as the case may be;
(d) the type of business and location;
(e) the number of persons employed or to be employed;
(f) the estimated annual income of the business;
(g) information to enable a determination of the level of exports, foreign
exchange earnings and foreign exchange savings, where applicable;
(h) the date of commencement of the business;
(i) any other information that is necessary to enable Cabinet to make a
decision.
6\. (1) Where an application is made to the Minister and Cabinet is satisfied that the
business:
(a) is a micro or small enterprise within the meaning of section 2 and;
(b) meets the other requirements for eligibility under this Act;
it shall grant to the business the status of approved small business.
(2) An approval granted by Cabinet under subsection (1) may, by Order of the
Minister, be published in the Gazette.
(3) Where Cabinet refuses to grant approval, the Minister shall in writing inform
the applicant of the reasons for the refusal.
7\. (1) A micro or small enterprise which is granted the status of an approved small
business shall submit to the Registrar:
(a) a copy of the application made in accordance with section 5; and
(b) evidence of the grant of the status of an approved small business.
(2) The Registrar shall keep a register to be known as “The Register of Approved
Small Businesses” in which shall be entered, upon receipt of the documentation referred
to in subsection (1) and the fee set out in the Third Schedule, the following:
(a) details of the name and the certificate of registration or incorporation
number of the approved small business issued under the Registration of
Business Names Act or the Companies Act, as the case may be;
(b) a number assigned by the Registrar to the approved small business
identifying it as an approved small business; and
(c) the particulars referred to in section 5 (3).
(3) The Registrar shall issue a certificate of registration in respect of every micro
or small enterprise registered under this Act in Form III of the Second Schedule, and the

certificate shall be conclusive proof of the registration of the approved small business
named in the certificate.
(4) A person may, in respect of the Register of Approved Small Businesses and
upon payment of the fee prescribed in the Third Schedule:
(a) inspect any document filed with the Registrar;
(b) request a certified extract or a certified or uncertified copy or extract of
any document filed with the Registrar.
8\. (1) Where after the grant of the status of approved small business, any change is
made or occurs in any of the particulars supplied to the Minister at the time of the
application for the status, the business shall, within 21 days after the change or such
longer period as the Minister may allow, forward to the Minister and the Registrar a
statement in writing in Form II of the Second Schedule specifying the nature and date of
the change.
(2) The Registrar shall, upon receipt of the statement referred to in subsection (1)
and the prescribed fee set out in the Third Schedule, enter the statement in the Register of
Approved Small Businesses.
9\. (1) Where:
(a) the particulars provided by an approved small business at the time of
making an application under section 5 (1) or at the time of registration
under section 7 were false in any material particular or were likely or
calculated to deceive;
(b) the approved small business fails or refuses to submit information to the
Minister and the Registrar pursuant to section 8 or interferes with or
prevents monitoring or inspection as provided under section 12;
(c) there is a failure on the part of the approved small business to:
(i) submit income tax returns as required under the Income Tax Act or
any other return required under any other relevant law,
(ii) pay national insurance contributions as required under the National
Insurance Act;
(d) the approved small business has not commenced operation within six
months of being granted the status of an approved small business;
(e) the approved small business fails to comply with any other conditions
attached to the grant of the approval;
Cabinet may, after giving written notice to the approved small business and an
opportunity to show cause why the approval should not be revoked, revoke the approval.
(2) Where an approved small business ceases to satisfy the requirements of this
Act in relation to its eligibility as a small business, it shall, by notice in writing, inform
Cabinet and Cabinet shall revoke the approval.

(3) The revocation of an approval under this Act shall not affect the rights or
liabilities of the business in respect of any obligation which existed before the revocation.
(4) Notwithstanding subsection (3), Cabinet, may, where it is satisfied that the
payment of any money due under that subsection would cause undue hardship, or that it
is expedient to do so, waive the whole or part of the money payable by the micro or small
enterprise.
(5) Where a grant of approval has been revoked under subsection (1) or (2):
(a) an order published under section 6 (2) shall cease to have effect; and
(b) the Minister shall forward notice of the revocation to the Registrar, who
shall strike the business from the Registrar with effect from the date of
revocation of the approval.
10\. (1) Notwithstanding any provisionsto the contrary contained in the relevant
enactment, an approved small business is entitled to one or more of the following
incentives subject to the conditions specified in this section:
(a) income tax concessions under the Income Tax Act at the rate of 20 per
cent on the gains or profits of the business;
(b) under the Income Tax Act, a deduction from assessable income of an
amount equal to 20 per cent of the actual expenditure incurred in respect
of the use of technology, market research and any other activity that is, in
the opinion of the Comptroller of Inland Revenue, directly related to the
development of the business;
(c) exemption from import duty on plant and equipment imported for use in
the business;
(d) exemption from the payment of stamp duty under the Stamp Duty Act on
all documents related to the business where the registration of the
documents is required by law;

(e) a deduction in the fees amounting to 20 per cent under the Trade Mark Act
or the Patents Act or both Acts on all documents related to the business
where registration of the documents is required by law.
(2) The deduction of tax specified in subsection (1) shall be limited to the tax
payable by the approved small business and no deduction shall be allowed to the extent
that the amount sought to be deducted is in excess of the tax that is payable by the
enterprise.
(3) All incentives granted to an approved small business under this section shall
terminate if the business ceases to qualify as an approved small business.
11\. (1) Cabinet may approve any government department, agency or private sector
organisation as a provider of technical assistance for the purpose of assisting approved
small businesses to commence, continue or expand operations.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1) technical assistance includes:
(a) the development of product design;
(b) the development of package and label design;
(c) the provision of assistance in the development and maintenance of quality
control;
(d) the provision of assistance to businesses seeking to participate in overseas
trade shows;
(e) the preparation of business plans to facilitate the initial financing and
management of the business;
(f) the provision of marketing assistance for the local and export market;
(g) the financing of feasibility studies relating to the development of new
businesses or products;
(h) the provision of assistance in managerial accounting or budgetary
analysis;
(i) the provision of assistance in general or production management.
12\. The Minister shall cause each approved small business to be monitored and
inspected to ensure that compliance by the micro or small enterprise complies with the
provisions of this Act, and for this purpose may:
(a) cause the books, records and accounts to be inspected; and
(b) request information that is necessary to enable the inspection to be carried
out.
13\. The Minister shall examine an approved small business annually for the purpose
of determining whether it continues to qualify as an approved small business.
14\. Where an approved small business ceases to conform to the requirements of this
Act , it shall, by notice in writing, within three months from the date of the cessation,
inform the Minister of the fact.
15\. Any money due by a small business under section 9 (3) may be recovered as a
debt due to the Crown in civil proceedings.
16\. There shall be payable to the Registrar in respect of the matters set out in the
Third Schedule the fees specified therein in relation to those matters.
17\. The Minister may:
(a) make Regulations to give effect to the provisions of this Act;
(b) by order amend the Schedules.

OBJECTS AND REASONS
The object of this Bill is to facilitate growth in the small business sector by providing
incentives for qualifying small businesses. The Bill sets out the regulatory framework
which will govern eligibility for the incentives and other pertinent matters.
